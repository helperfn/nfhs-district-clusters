# Cluster profiles - kmeans_pca, k = 8

Domain scores are mean z-scores with every indicator oriented so that **positive = better than the national average**.

## Cluster 0 - Middle development, strong clean energy

- **Districts:** 42
- **States most represented:** Telangana (29), Andhra Pradesh (13)
- **Mean composite score:** 0.590 (national mean 0.594)
- **Strengths:** Energy (+1.00), Population & household (+0.39), Water & sanitation (WASH) (+0.37)
- **Gaps:** Education (-0.40)
- **Held-out outcomes (never used to build the cluster):** stunting 32.2%, underweight 31.5%, child anaemia 68.0%, women's anaemia 58.5%
- **Most typical districts:** Bhadradri Kothagudem (Telangana), Peddapalli (Telangana), Siddipet (Telangana), Yadadri Bhuvanagiri (Telangana), Kamareddy (Telangana)
- **Policy focus:** Priority: girls' schooling and adult literacy; then blood pressure / blood sugar screening.
